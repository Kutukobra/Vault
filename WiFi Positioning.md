### Methods
- [[RSSI]] has been widely studied for indoor positioning due to its availability.
- Geometric methods with trilateration path models are used but suffers from noise.
- Fingerprinting with machine learning improves performance moderately.
- Deep learning ([[RNN]], [[CNNLoc]]) enhances feature extraction but do not meet precision for UAV.
- [[CSI]] with subcarrier-level frequency domain data offers finer propagation analysis, outperforming [[RSSI]].
### CSI For Positioning
- Distance estimation mitigates multipath effects ([[FILA]], [[LiFS]]).
- [[AoA]] enhances precision but requires specialized hardwares ([[ArrayTrack]], [[SpotFi]])
- [[Machine Learning]] fingerprinting systems ([[DeepFi]], [[CiFi]]) extracts [[CSI]] features for superior 2D scenarios.
### Challenges for CSI-Based UAV Localization
- [[Multipath Propagation]].
- High-precision 3D resolution.
- Robustness in dynamic settings.
