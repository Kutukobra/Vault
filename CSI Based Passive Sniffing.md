- Indoor drones typically uses [[WiFi]] for monitoring and controlling flights (sensor and command transfer).
- WiFi in indoor spaces facilitates CSI collection from UAVs. 
- Passive Sniffing collects CSI without requiring UAV communication links.
- ICMP echo requests to extract CSI from reply packets to have non-intrusive data acquisition.
- IWL5300 & Atheros WiFi cards rely on outdated configs.
- Nexmon firmware requires spesific WiFi chips, limited practicallity.
- Traditional passive methods struggle with [[Multipath Propagation|multipath propagation]], frequency offsets, and 3D analysis.
